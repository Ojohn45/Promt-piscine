# Prompt:
        "Write an email inviting a candidate to an interview"

### Friendly Tone:
Dear Joy,
We are excited to invite you to interview for the Software Developer position at Learn2earn Tech!

Your interview is scheduled for Monday, May 4th at 10:00 AM via Zoom. It will last about 45 minutes, and you will be meeting with Mr. Kinsley, our Team Lead.

Please have your resume and portfolio ready. There may also be a short coding exercise during the session.

Kindly reply to confirm your availability.

We look forward to speaking with you!

Best regards,
Odumu John
HR Manager
Learn2earn Tech
        
### Professional Tone:
Dear Joy,

We are pleased to invite you to attend an interview for the Software Developer position at Learn2earn Tech.

The interview has been scheduled for Monday, May 4th at 10:00 AM via Zoom and will last approximately 45 minutes. You will be meeting with Mr. Kinsley, our Team Lead.

Kindly ensure that your resume and portfolio are available during the interview. Please note that you may also be required to complete a brief coding exercise.

Please confirm your availability by replying to this email.

We look forward to your response.

Yours sincerely,
Odumu John
HR Manager
Learn2earn Tech

# Question3 = Compare the outputs and note how the tone changes while the content stays consistent.

So in the Friendly Tone version it is more casual, conversational and more approachable. While Professional Tone is more formal and academical than Friendly Tone it also sound like a question asked from a text book or exam, and it encourages seriousnes and ethical response.
