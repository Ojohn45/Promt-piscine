# Objective: Practice creative writing through prompts.

### Question1 = Choose a theme (e.g., "friendship," "space exploration," "overcoming fear").
Friendship.

### Question2 = Write a simple generative prompt: "Write a short story about [theme] in under 300 words."
promt: Write a very short story about Freindship in under 100 words.

Model output: When Tunde’s bicycle broke halfway up the dusty hill, he almost cried. He’d saved for months to buy it. Before he could give up, Zara appeared beside him, laughing softly. “We’ll push it together,” she said.

They struggled, slipping and sweating under the hot sun, but neither let go. By the time they reached the top, the sunset painted the sky gold. The bicycle was still broken, but somehow it didn’t matter.

Some things are easier to fix than others. Friendship isn’t one of them — it just works, especially when the road gets steep.

### Question3 = Test with at least two different themes and compare the style of outputs.
Promt: Write a very short story about Displine in under 50 words.

Model: Every morning, Ada woke before sunrise to practice the piano. Some days she felt tired; some days she felt bored. Still, she played. Months later, when her fingers danced confidently across the keys, she realized discipline isn’t loud or flashy — it’s quiet effort repeated daily.

Promt: Write a very short story about why it is good to be Happy in under 50 words.

Model output: Chidi used to chase success like it was a finish line. One afternoon, he paused to laugh with friends under a mango tree. The world felt lighter. He learned happiness doesn’t slow you down — it fuels you, clears your mind, and makes every small victory sweeter.
